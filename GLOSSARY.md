# ATR in Ticks glossary

These terms describe the indicator's volatility reading and units.

## Language

**True range**:
A bar's price range that also accounts for gaps from the previous close.

**Average true range (ATR)**:
A smoothed series of true-range values over a selected length.

**Tick**:
A symbol's minimum price increment, used here to express a price-range reading.

**Tick value**:
The monetary value of one tick for a particular instrument and position size.
It is distinct from the tick's price increment.
